# Device atlas coverage

Open **Device catalog & components** on the Overview page. The atlas works offline and supports Arabic/English and light/dark modes.

42 manufacturer entries, 110 family matching rules and 25 bundled illustrations. This is a maintained directory, not a claim to cover every manufacturer, SKU, enclosure or internal component ever produced. A family match does not certify an exact exterior or teardown. Only the existing ASUS FA607NUG exterior photo has an exact-model source.

## How matching works

Manufacturer aliases are matched first. Windows enclosure type and PCSystemType distinguish laptops, towers, mini PCs, all-in-ones, workstations and detachables. Model, SystemFamily and ComputerSystemProduct.Version identify the family, including Lenovo systems with opaque model codes. Unknown models receive an explicitly generic form-factor illustration. A manual artwork choice never changes detected identity or specifications. All pictures are bundled; device identifiers are not sent to image services.

## Components

The 12 categories are CPU, graphics, memory, motherboard, physical storage, displays, network, audio, battery, case, cooling and power supply. Available entries come from local Windows inventory. Multiple GPUs, RAM modules and drives remain separate. Manufacturer names may be missing or firmware placeholders. Physical drive capacity is shown in binary GB units; interface names are reported by Windows and are not inferred NVMe/SSD claims. Monitor entries may be generic.

Windows WMI does not reliably identify retail cases, cooling assemblies or power supplies. These categories say that their models cannot be reported. Reference-brand lists are examples, never an assertion that those parts are installed or compatible. No serial numbers, MAC addresses or IP addresses are added by these new inventory queries.
